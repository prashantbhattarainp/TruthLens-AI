# Known Limitations

- TruthLens AI provides an automated signal, not a factual verdict or a guarantee of accuracy.
- The system does not validate the credibility of a publisher, author, image, URL, or external source.
- The current public workflow accepts article text only; URL analysis is not yet available.
- Explanations describe factors that influenced an automated classification. They do not prove causation or factual truth.
- v1.0.0 is stateless: it does not provide user accounts, saved predictions, analytics history, or a database.
- Performance depends on service availability and the locally configured ML runtime package.
- Important legal, medical, financial, safety, or civic decisions require qualified advice and reliable independent sources.
