Research programme

Explainable fake news detection for Indian digital media

TruthLens AI is being developed as a reproducible research platform with explicit attention to data provenance, evaluation, model transparency, and future language coverage.

Research objective

Study useful, accountable AI assistance

The research objective is to evaluate whether explainable NLP and machine-learning methods can support reliable credibility assessment for Indian digital-news content.

The platform will foreground comparability and limitations: a prediction alone is insufficient without a documented dataset, clear evaluation protocol, and interpretable evidence.

Planned workstreams

Research components to be developed incrementally

Dataset strategy · placeholder

Indian news data curation

Define source criteria, labelling policy, licence checks, class balance, split strategy, and dataset-version records before model training begins.

ML pipeline · placeholder

Comparable model baselines

Establish configurable spaCy preprocessing and replaceable feature extraction so TF-IDF baselines can later be compared with embedding-based approaches.

Future experiments · placeholder

Evaluation beyond accuracy

Plan for precision, recall, F1, ROC analysis, confusion matrices, error analysis, training time, and controlled hyperparameter comparisons.

Explainability · placeholder

Interpretable model outputs

Present important textual signals and progressively evaluate explanation methods such as feature attribution, LIME, and SHAP where appropriate.

Research readiness

Every future experiment should be traceable

Dataset versions, preprocessing settings, model metadata, hyperparameters, and saved evaluation reports are planned as core platform records.
