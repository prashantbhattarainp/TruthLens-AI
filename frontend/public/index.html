Project purpose

Evidence-led assistance for a complex information space

TruthLens AI is being designed as a research platform—not a simplistic true-or-false tool. It will bring structured preprocessing, baseline models, transparent outputs, and experiment records into one extensible system.

The initial scope prioritizes Indian digital media and creates room for future multilingual analysis, stronger language models, and reproducible evaluation.

Research motivation

Trustworthy AI needs more than a label

News classification is only useful when its limits, rationale, and evaluation are visible. The platform is structured to make those research questions first-class.

Indian media context

Dataset and evaluation planning begin with Indian news sources, their formats, and the path toward regional-language coverage.

Explainable decisions

Future outputs will present confidence, important terms, model details, and a clear disclaimer instead of an unexplained verdict.

Reproducible experiments

Versioned datasets, models, metrics, and reports will enable comparison across experiments and support future research publication.
