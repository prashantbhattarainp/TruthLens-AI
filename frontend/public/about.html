About the platform

Building a credible foundation for AI and media research

TruthLens AI connects a usable web experience with independent platform and machine learning services, keeping research workflows clear, maintainable, and extensible.

Project vision

Move from a classifier demo to an accountable platform

The aim is not to represent automated classification as a final fact-check. It is to build a transparent research tool that reports a model’s assessment, supporting evidence, confidence, and limitations for careful human interpretation.

Initial work focuses on Indian digital media, while the structure is designed to accommodate multilingual research and future production-quality capabilities.

Technologies

Deliberately simple foundations, clear extension points

Web interface

HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, and Chart.js will keep the experience lightweight and directly understandable.

Platform service

Node.js, Express, and SQLite provide a clear starting point for APIs, activity records, and later authentication.

ML service

Python, spaCy, scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy, and joblib will support reproducible classical-model experiments.

Development methodology

Incremental delivery with evidence at each step

The project advances in small, reviewable milestones. Each milestone defines a narrow goal, testable output, recorded engineering decisions, and one focused commit.

Research-sensitive choices—such as dataset policy, preprocessing, and evaluation protocol—will be recorded separately from routine engineering progress so the eventual paper has a dependable evidence trail.
